# Esports web: brand notes

Reference study for `packages/web-esports`, taken from the live FaZe Vegas
(fazevegas.com) and Gentle Mates (gentlemates.com) sites in October 2026.

## FaZe Vegas

- Pure black canvas, one saturated magenta, white. No second hue anywhere in
  the interface; color variety lives in photography and kits.
- Magenta is a signal, not a surface: the mark, the one CTA pill, a single
  word. Big areas stay black.
- The mark carries the energy: a forward-slanted, angular "FV" chevron with a
  white keyline, like a sticker. Speed comes from the angle, not from glows or
  gradients.
- Tall condensed uppercase headings (Anton-like), set upright. Short body
  statements in a geometric sans, uppercase.
- The logo tiled at very low contrast works as background texture.
- Insider voice in few words: "LOCK IN", "SZN", "#FAZEUP".

## Gentle Mates

- Reads as a streetwear house, not a gamer brand. Off-white, cobalt, navy,
  brown, lavender, with lime on some drops.
- A small crest monogram (the laureled "M8"), centered and quiet. Confidence
  comes from restraint.
- Small, widely tracked uppercase labels ("WORLDWIDE" under the logotype),
  hairline dividers, a grid of equal tiles, generous empty space.
- Logotypes change per collection; jerseys (pinstripes, crests) are treated
  as design objects.

## Why the first pass felt tacky

- Pink everywhere at once, with no hierarchy: offset pink shadows on every
  button, pink-tinted panels, a pink ticker band.
- A blue-black canvas plus pink-tinted washes read muddy and synthwave.
- The sports system's hard offset shadows were recolored, not rethought.
- Square, techy letterforms across the whole interface read as a gamer
  template.
- The wordmark lockup was identical to sports.

## Direction

- Neutral true black and off-white, with greys for surfaces. Pink only on the
  primary action, the selected state, one accent word, and focus.
- Flat fills and white keylines instead of offset shadows.
- Forward slant as the scene's tell: primary actions and selected pills are
  parallelograms, like broadcast score bugs.
- Gentle Mates restraint: small tracked labels, hairlines, equal tiles, more
  black space.
- Kit pinstripes as the only texture, at very low contrast.
- A wordmark lockup of its own: the name in a slanted heavy cut over a small,
  widely tracked "today".
- Implicit scene language ("live", "lock in", "queue up"); never "esports" or
  "gamer" in the interface. No added copy.
